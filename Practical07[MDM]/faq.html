Frequently Asked Questions

Click on a question to find the answer.

Students generally need their mark sheets, transfer certificate, identity proof, passport-size photographs, and other documents mentioned by the college during the admission process.

You can check your admission status through the college admission portal or contact the admission office with your application or registration details.

Fees can usually be paid through the college's online payment portal or through the payment methods provided by the accounts department.

Scholarship eligibility depends on the scholarship scheme and its requirements. Students should check the official scholarship guidelines and submit the required documents before the deadline.

Contact the concerned college department as soon as possible. They can guide you regarding document correction or resubmission.

After successful fee payment, the admission receipt can generally be downloaded from the student portal or collected from the accounts or admission office.
